Project Exodus v31 - Supplied GLB Hands

- Replaced the procedural first-person hand meshes with the exact user-supplied Meshy hand GLB.
- The supplied asset is loaded once because it contains the complete two-hand model.
- Both left and right hands therefore remain part of the same authored model and cannot diverge/disappear from mirrored procedural placement.
- Existing rifle, pistol, shotgun, firing, recoil, scope, inventory and world systems are retained.
- The old procedural hand entities remain only as invisible animation anchors to avoid breaking existing gameplay code.
